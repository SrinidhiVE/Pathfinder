      How Pathfinder reached this result

      Your answers→ Your 8 strength dimensions→ Stream compatibility→ Your result

      Each of your 15 answers is weighted into eight dimensions (for example, Analytical Thinking or Communication). Each stream then counts some dimensions more than others, and your alignment is the weighted average. It is a transparent, rule-based method — no hidden model — and only your answers are used, never personal identity details.
